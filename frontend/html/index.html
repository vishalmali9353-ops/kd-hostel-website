About Our Hostel

Your Home Away From Home

K.D. Hostel, near K. D. Polytechnic, Patan, provides separate hostel accommodation for boys and girls. The hostel aims to support students with essential amenities and an environment focused on study, discipline and student welfare.

  • Separate Boys and Girls Hostels
  • Study and Reading Room
  • RO Drinking Water
  • CCTV and Security Guards
