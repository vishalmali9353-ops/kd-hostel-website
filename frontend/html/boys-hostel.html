About the Facility

About Boys Hostel

K. D. Hostel is located near K. D. Polytechnic on Hemchandracharya North Gujarat University Road, Patan. The boys hostel provides accommodation for students and aims to support their academic life.

Students should contact the Rector or the respective Warden for current room availability, hostel rules and admission requirements.
