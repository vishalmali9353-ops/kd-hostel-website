About K.D. Hostel

A place to live, learn and grow.

Welcome to K.D. Hostel

K.D. Hostel is located near K. D. Polytechnic, on Hemchandracharya North Gujarat University Road, Patan, Gujarat.

The hostel provides student accommodation, study spaces, RO drinking water, recreation facilities and campus security arrangements.

Admission forms are available from the Rector/Warden office. Room allotment is subject to applicable institute and government hostel norms.
