Boys Hostel

About Boys Hostel

K. D. Polytechnic College provides safe, comfortable, and student-friendly hostel facilities for diploma engineering students. Separate hostel accommodations are available for boys and girls with well-maintained rooms and essential amenities. The hostel environment promotes discipline, cultural harmony, and mutual cooperation among students. Proper administration, cleanliness, security, and student welfare are ensured through continuous monitoring by the hostel authorities.

Hostel Facilities

  • Well-furnished rooms
  • 24/7 RO drinking water facility
  • Separate study/reading room
  • TV and entertainment room
  • Solar water heater
  • CCTV surveillance and security guards
  • Clean and well-maintained campus and garden

Hostel Administration

The hostel administration is supervised by the Hostel Rector and Wardens appointed by the institute administration for effective management and student support.
