Fee Management

Tuition Fee Payment

Students can easily pay their tuition and hostel fees online using the college's digital payment portal, powered by Qfix. Simply visit the payment link below, enter your student details, select the fee category, and choose your preferred payment method — UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking. After completing the transaction, make sure to download or save the payment receipt for future reference. This online system is fast, secure, and available 24/7 for all students.

PAYMENT LINK :- https://www.eduqfix.com/PayDirect/#/student
Pay Fees via Qfix

How to Pay

  1. Click on the Payment Link above to open the Qfix portal.
  2. Enter your student details (name, roll number, admission ID).
  3. Select the correct fee category (Tuition / Hostel / Mess).
  4. Choose a payment method: UPI, debit/credit card, or net banking.
  5. Complete the payment and download the receipt for your records.
