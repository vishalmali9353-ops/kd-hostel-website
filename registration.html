Student Registration

Fill in accurate details below to register for a hostel seat at K.D. Hostel, Patan.

All submissions are reviewed by the hostel administration office.

Before You Begin

  • Student must be at least 14 years of age on the date of registration.
  • Student must currently be enrolled in a recognized course / branch.
  • All fields marked with an asterisk in the form below are mandatory.

  • Aadhar Card / Government issued ID card.
  • Latest college / school enrollment or admission proof.
  • Passport size photograph (at the time of physical verification).

The information submitted here is stored securely and is used only by the K.D. Hostel administration for registration and emergency contact purposes.
